What is Color Screen with Timer?

Color Screen with Timer is a free, easy-to-use online tool that transforms your screen into a vibrant fullscreen color display with an optional countdown timer. Whether you need ambient lighting, a focus timer, or a professional backdrop, our tool provides a simple yet powerful solution that works on any device.

How It Works?

Color Screen with Timer is designed to be simple and intuitive. Here's how each feature works:

  • Choose Your Color: Select from 8 preset colors (Blue, Green, Red, Black, Yellow, Orange, Pink, Purple) with a single click, or use the color picker to choose any custom color you want
  • Set Your Timer: Enter a time duration in minutes (or leave blank for no timer), then click "Start Timer" to begin the countdown
  • Go Fullscreen: Click the "Go Fullscreen" button or press F to enter fullscreen mode - your entire screen transforms into your selected color
  • Timer Display Options: Toggle "Show timer when in fullscreen" to display or hide the countdown while in fullscreen mode, and choose where the timer appears (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, or bottom-right)
  • Sound Alerts: Select an alert sound (Bell, Chime, or Beep) to play when your timer ends, or choose "None" for silent operation
  • Keyboard Controls: Use Space to start/pause the timer, R to reset it, and F to toggle fullscreen - no mouse needed
  • Language Support: Switch between 13 languages using the language dropdown menu - your preference is saved automatically
  • Saved Preferences: Your color choice, timer settings, and language preference are saved in cookies so they're ready next time you visit

Key Features

  • Fullscreen Color Display: Choose from multiple preset colors or create your own custom shade using the color picker
  • Countdown Timer: Set customizable timers for focus sessions, breaks, or time management
  • Timer Overlay: Display the countdown timer while in fullscreen mode with customizable positioning
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Quick access with Space (start/pause), R (reset), and F (fullscreen)

Use Cases

Photography & Videography

Use color screens as lighting sources for creative photography and video production. Different colors create different moods and effects:

  • Blue Screen: Professional backdrop for compositing and chroma key work
  • Green Screen: Industry-standard for video effects and background replacement
  • Warm Colors: Create golden hour lighting effects indoors
  • Cool Colors: Add dramatic or moody lighting to your shots

Video Conferencing & Remote Work

Improve your video call appearance with professional lighting:

  • Zoom Lighting: Soft, warm lighting to enhance your face during video calls
  • Fill Light: Reduce shadows and create even lighting for professional meetings
  • Color Backgrounds: Create unique and eye-catching backgrounds for virtual presentations

Focus & Productivity

Use the timer features to implement proven productivity techniques:

  • Pomodoro Technique: 25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute breaks
  • Time Blocking: Dedicate specific time blocks to different tasks
  • Study Sessions: Set timers for focused learning periods
  • Break Reminders: Regular breaks to prevent burnout and maintain productivity

Meditation & Relaxation

Create calming environments with soothing colors:

  • Blue & Purple: Calming colors for meditation and relaxation
  • Timed Sessions: Set meditation durations with gentle audio notifications
  • Ambient Lighting: Soft background lighting for yoga and mindfulness practices

Screen Testing & Calibration

Test your display for dead pixels, color accuracy, and uniformity:

  • Solid Colors: Identify dead or stuck pixels on your screen
  • Color Accuracy: Test color reproduction and calibration
  • Burn-in Testing: Check for screen burn-in issues

Get Started

Ready to transform your screen? Getting started is easy:

  1. Visit our home page
  2. Click on a preset color button (Blue, Green, Red, Yellow, etc.) or use the color picker to choose a custom color
  3. Optionally set a timer duration in minutes and choose your preferred alert sound
  4. Click "Go Fullscreen" or press F on your keyboard to enter fullscreen mode
  5. Use the keyboard shortcuts (Space to pause/resume, R to reset) to control your timer
  6. Press Esc or click "Exit Fullscreen" when you're done

That's all there is to it! Your preferences (color, timer settings, language) will be saved automatically for your next visit.
