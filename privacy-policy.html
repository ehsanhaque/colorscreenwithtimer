TL;DR - Our Promise to You: We use essential cookies to remember your preferences (language, timer settings, color choices) and optional cookies for analytics and advertising with your consent. We don't collect or store personal information. You have full control over which cookies to accept.

Introduction

At Color Screen with Timer (colorscreenwithtimer.com), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle (or rather, don't handle) your data when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

Essential Cookies (Required)

We use essential first-party cookies to provide basic functionality and remember your preferences:

  • Language Preference: Remembers your selected language (13 languages available)
  • Color Selection: Saves your last chosen color for convenience
  • Timer Settings: Stores your timer duration, sound preferences, and display position
  • Cookie Consent: Remembers your cookie preferences and consent choices

These cookies are stored locally in your browser and contain no personally identifiable information. They expire after 365 days.

Analytics Cookies (Optional - Requires Consent)

If enabled, and with your consent, we may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website:

  • Usage Statistics: Page views, session duration, bounce rates
  • Traffic Sources: How visitors find our website (search engines, direct links, etc.)
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution (anonymized)
  • Geographic Data: Country and city-level location data (no precise location tracking)

Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect IP addresses, but we have enabled IP anonymization. You can opt out of analytics cookies through our cookie preferences.

Advertising Cookies (Optional - Requires Consent)

With your consent, we display advertisements through Google AdSense to support this free tool:

  • Personalized Ads: Google AdSense may use cookies to show relevant advertisements based on your interests
  • Ad Performance: Tracking which ads are displayed and clicked (no personal data collected by us)
  • Remarketing: Google may show ads for our website on other sites you visit

You can opt out of advertising cookies through our cookie preferences or visit Google Ads Settings.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we don't collect:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information (except through our contact form)
  • No User Accounts: We don't require registration or account creation to use the tool
  • No Payment Information: We never collect credit cards or financial data - our tool is completely free
  • No Precise Location: We do not track your GPS coordinates or precise location
  • No Microphone/Camera Access: We never request access to your device's camera or microphone

How Our Tool Works

Color Screen with Timer runs entirely in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • All core functionality (color display, timer) happens locally on your device
  • Your preferences are stored in first-party cookies
  • The tool works offline once the page is loaded (except for ads and analytics)
  • No user-generated content is stored on our servers

Contact Form Data

If you use our contact form to reach out to us:

  • Email Address: We collect your email address to respond to your inquiry
  • Message Content: We store your message to address your questions or concerns
  • Retention: Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry
  • Usage: This information is used solely to communicate with you and is never shared with third parties

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics

If enabled, and with your consent, we may use Google Analytics to track website usage and improve our service. Google Analytics may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser and device information
  • Anonymized IP addresses
  • Referral sources (how you found our website)

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy | Opt-out of Google Analytics

Google AdSense

With your consent, we display advertisements through Google AdSense to support this free tool. Google AdSense may:

  • Use cookies to show personalized advertisements based on your interests
  • Track ad impressions and clicks
  • Share data with advertising partners in the Google Display Network

Learn more: How Google uses data | Ad Settings

Hosting Services

Our website is hosted on servers that may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes. This logging is performed by our hosting provider, not by us, and we do not have direct access to or use this information.

reCAPTCHA

Our contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to prevent spam and abuse. reCAPTCHA may collect:

  • IP addresses
  • Mouse movements and keyboard patterns
  • Browser and device information

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Our service does not require any personal information and is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

International Users

Our website can be accessed from anywhere in the world. Since we don't collect any personal data, there are no cross-border data transfer concerns.

Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data and privacy:

  • Cookie Preferences: Manage your cookie consent at any time through our cookie preferences page
  • Clear Cookies: Delete all cookies through your browser settings to remove stored preferences
  • Opt-out of Analytics: Disable Google Analytics through our cookie settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: Disable advertising cookies through our cookie settings or visit Google Ads Settings
  • Private Browsing: Use browser privacy features like incognito/private mode to prevent cookie storage
  • Contact Form Data: If you've contacted us, email us to request deletion of your contact information

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Lawful Basis: Essential cookies use "legitimate interest," while analytics and advertising require your explicit consent
  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of data collected through our contact form
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of contact form data at any time
  • Right to Object: You can opt out of analytics and advertising cookies
  • Data Portability: Contact form data can be provided in a machine-readable format upon request
  • Transparent Processing: This privacy policy clearly explains all data collection and processing

CCPA Compliance (California Residents)

We comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell your personal information to third parties
  • Right to Know: You have the right to know what personal information we collect (detailed in this policy)
  • Right to Delete: You can request deletion of your contact form submissions
  • Right to Opt-out: You can opt out of analytics and advertising cookies at any time
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

Data Retention

We retain different types of data for different periods:

  • Essential Cookies: 365 days from last visit
  • Analytics Cookies: Up to 26 months (Google Analytics default)
  • Advertising Cookies: Varies by ad network (typically 30-90 days)
  • Contact Form Data: Retained until inquiry is resolved, then deleted unless legally required to retain

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please visit our contact page.

Summary

Bottom Line: We use essential cookies to make the tool work better for you (saving your language, colors, and timer preferences). Optional analytics and advertising cookies require your consent and can be disabled anytime. We don't collect personal information except through the contact form, and we never sell your data. You have full control over your privacy through our cookie preferences.
