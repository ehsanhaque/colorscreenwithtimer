Free Fullscreen Color Screen with a Countdown Timer

Color Screen with Timer turns your display into a clean, fullscreen block of solid color with an optional countdown timer built in. Choose a preset such as a white screen, black screen, red screen, green screen or blue screen, or use the custom color picker to set any exact shade, then go fullscreen for a distraction-free, edge-to-edge color display. It runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no sign-up, and it works offline.

How to Use the Color Screen and Timer

1. Pick a color

Click a preset color button or open the custom color picker to dial in any hex color you need.

2. Go fullscreen

Press the Fullscreen button or tap the F key to fill your entire screen with the color.

3. Set an optional countdown timer

Enter hours, minutes and seconds, choose an alert sound, and start the timer with the Space key. You can keep the timer visible in fullscreen and choose exactly where it appears on screen.

Popular Ways to Use a Fullscreen Color Screen

Video call & Zoom lighting

Use a warm white or the Zoom preset on a second screen or phone as a soft fill light that brightens your face on Zoom, Teams and Google Meet.

Turn a tablet or monitor into an adjustable colored light source for portraits, product shots and creative color effects.

Dead pixel & backlight bleed test

Cycle through solid white, black, red, green and blue in fullscreen to spot dead or stuck pixels and check a monitor for backlight bleed. See the full dead pixel test guide.

Mood & ambient lighting

Fill a room with calming color, set a relaxing scene, or use a bright white screen as an impromptu light panel or lightbox.

Focus & Pomodoro sessions

Pair a solid background with the countdown timer to run Pomodoro focus blocks, study sprints, workouts or short breaks.

Presentations & events

Show a clean color backdrop with a visible countdown for talks, workshops, classroom activities and stage timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Color Screen with Timer free?

Yes. It is completely free with no registration or installation. It runs in any modern browser on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile, and works offline once loaded.

How do I use a color screen for Zoom or video call lighting?

Open the tool on a second screen, phone or tablet, select a warm white or the Zoom preset, and go fullscreen. Point the glowing screen toward your face to act as a soft, even fill light on video calls.

How do I test my monitor for dead pixels or backlight bleed?

Enter fullscreen and cycle through white, black, red, green and blue. A dead or stuck pixel appears as a spot that stays the wrong color, while backlight bleed shows up as lighter patches near the edges on a solid black screen.

Does the countdown timer work in fullscreen?

Yes. Set hours, minutes and seconds, pick an alert sound, and keep the timer visible in fullscreen. You can choose where the timer overlay appears or hide it entirely.

Which keyboard shortcuts are available?

Press Space to start or pause the timer, R to reset it, and F to toggle fullscreen.
