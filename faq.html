Frequently Asked Questions

Is Color Screen with Timer really free?

Yes! It's completely free to use with no hidden costs, subscriptions, or premium features. We may display ads to help cover hosting costs, but the tool itself is 100% free.

Do I need to create an account?

No, you don't need to register or create an account. Just visit the website and start using the tool immediately.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once the page is loaded, the tool works completely offline. Your settings are stored locally in your browser.

What browsers are supported?

Color Screen with Timer works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera on desktop, mobile, and tablet devices.

How do I use keyboard shortcuts?

Press Space to start/pause the timer, R to reset, and F to toggle fullscreen mode.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes! The tool is released under the MIT License, which allows both personal and commercial use. See our License page for details.

Do you collect my data?

No, we don't collect any personal data or track your usage. Your privacy is important to us. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

The timer isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the problem persists, please email us with details about your browser and the issue you're experiencing.

Can I customize the colors beyond the presets?

Yes! Use the custom color picker on the home page to select any color you want. You can create millions of different colors.

How do I exit fullscreen mode?

Press the F key, press Esc, or click the fullscreen button again to exit fullscreen mode.
