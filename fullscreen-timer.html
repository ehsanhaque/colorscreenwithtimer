Fullscreen Timer: Free Online Full Screen Countdown Timer

A fullscreen timer shows a large, clearly visible countdown that fills your entire screen — ideal when an audience, class or team needs to see the time from across a room. This full screen countdown timer lets you choose any background color, pick an alert sound and go fullscreen in one click. It runs in your browser with no download and no sign-up, on any laptop, monitor, tablet or projector.

How to Use the Fullscreen Countdown Timer

  1. Set the time. Enter hours, minutes and seconds for your countdown.
  2. Choose a background color. Pick a preset or a custom shade so the timer stands out for your audience.
  3. Go fullscreen. Press the F key or the fullscreen button so the timer fills the whole screen.
  4. Start the countdown. Press Space to start or pause, and R to reset. Choose an alert sound for when time runs out.
  5. Position the timer. Show it in the center or any corner, or hide the overlay so only the color shows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the fullscreen timer free?

Yes. The fullscreen countdown timer is completely free with no registration or installation, and runs in any modern browser on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile.

Can I change the timer's background color?

Yes. You can set any preset or custom color as the background so the timer is easy to read from across a room or on a projector.

Does the timer play a sound when it finishes?

Yes. You can choose from several alert sounds — beep, ding, chime, buzzer or ping — or turn the sound off entirely.

Where can I position the timer on screen?

You can show the countdown in the center or in any corner while in fullscreen, or hide the overlay so only the background color shows.
